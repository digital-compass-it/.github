# 🧭 Digital Compass IT
### Honest IT Career Orientation, Step-by-Step Roadmaps & Free Industry Certifications

Welcome to **Digital Compass IT** – a practical, no-nonsense guide for career changers who want to enter the tech industry **without wasting time or money on low-quality schools**.

---

### 💡 Why This Project Exists
After **30+ years of real-world experience in the IT industry** and teaching career orientation courses, I have seen the same problem over and over again:
1. **Lack of Orientation:** Career changers often have only a vague idea of what different IT roles actually look like, which skills they require, and whether a role truly fits their personality.
2. **Broken Promises by Schools:** Many bootcamps and training providers are funded "per head" rather than by student success. They sell generic, overpriced courses that fail to prepare you for real-world jobs.
3. **Hidden Goldmines:** At the same time, leading tech companies (especially Microsoft, ISTQB, and open-source communities) offer **free, high-quality training and recognized certifications** – if you know where to look and in what order to take them.

**My mission is simple:** Bring light into the dark, give you honest roadmaps for specific IT roles, and show you how to build job-ready skills step by step.
